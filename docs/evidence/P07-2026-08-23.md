## Deterministic model and failure contract

- Baseline source is a 180 Hz, 1 V-peak, zero-phase cosine with 1.65 V offset; sample rate is 1000 sample/s,
  count is 51, first-order cutoff is 400 Hz, ADC is 12-bit over 3.3 V, and deadline is infinite.
- The steady-state analog component response is calculated before sampling as `H(f)=1/(1+j f/f_c)`, with
  explicit magnitude and phase. Startup transient and initial filter state are excluded. The bypass fault
  removes that analog response; no digital filter is mislabeled as anti-aliasing.
- Sample instants are `t_n=n/f_s`, the strict rule is `f_in<f_s/2`, and signed folding is
  `mod(f_in+f_s/2,f_s)-f_s/2`. Exact Nyquist equality remains unsafe and phase-sensitive.
- Baseline Nyquist frequency is 500 Hz, margin is 320 Hz, apparent frequency is 180 Hz, and raw unpacked
  traffic is 12000 bit/s. Observation span is 50 ms; the declared `N/f_s` acquisition budget is 51 ms.
- The ideal ADC convention is `L=2^B`, `LSB=V_ref/L`, floor quantization of clipped values, endpoint code
  saturation, and code-center voltage reconstruction. Quantization, clipping, and aliasing remain distinct.
- Sample-rate sweep `[1000 600 400 300 250]` retains source/front-end/ADC settings and produces signed folds
  `[180 180 180 -120 -70]` Hz, magnitudes `[180 180 180 120 70]` Hz, and strict-safe flags
  `[true true true false false]`.
- Input-frequency sweep `[50 180 350 500 650 820 1150]` resets sampling to 1000 sample/s and produces
  apparent frequencies `[50 180 350 500 350 180 150]` Hz. Analog gain changes separately with physical
  frequency; a finite first-order response attenuates but never makes above-Nyquist content strictly safe.
- With the analog path bypassed, separate 180 Hz and 820 Hz zero-phase cosines at 1 ksample/s have different
  dense physical traces but identical canonical sample voltages and ADC codes. Increasing ADC resolution does
  not recover the physical source frequency.
- Exact ADC rails map to endpoint codes without an overrange flag; a separate 2 V-peak fixture crosses both
  rails and retains bounded codes. That failure is clipping, not frequency folding.
- A deadline exactly at `N/f_s` is accepted; one representable step earlier and zero are rejected with no
  committed codes. This analytical classification does not stop a converter, DMA engine, or bus driver.
- Calls are state-free; clean inputs are exact rollback, clean re-invocation is recovery, and malformed calls
  cannot contaminate later calls. There is no asynchronous cancellation operation.
