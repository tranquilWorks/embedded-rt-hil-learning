## Deterministic model and failure contract

- Baseline expected payload is `[0xA3 0x56 0xC9 0x1E]`, register `0x2D`, I2C seven-bit address `0x48`,
  equal 400 kHz configured clocks, no fault, no stretch, and an infinite deterministic deadline.
- The declared SPI fixture holds chip select across mode-0, MSB-first command `0xAD` and four dummy bytes.
  MOSI and MISO shift simultaneously, so `C_spi = 8(N+1)`: 40 pulses, 100 us, and 80% payload efficiency.
- I2C sends address-write `0x90`, register `0x2D`, repeated START, address-read `0x91`, and data. Each byte
  has a ninth response, so `C_i2c = 9(N+3)`: 63 pulses, 157.5 us, and `32/63` payload efficiency.
- START, repeated START, and STOP remain boundary positions, not invented SCL data pulses. Their physical
  setup/hold and bus-free timing is omitted; time is named clocked or modeled elapsed time.
- The target owns the first three I2C ACKs. The controller owns data responses and intentionally NACKs the
  final byte; this is successful termination rather than a target failure.
- The comparable register-read fixture requires at least one returned byte. Zero-length model and scenario
  requests are rejected rather than reporting a completed read with no controller-owned final NACK.
- Payload-length sweep `[1 2 4 8 16]` retains clock/address/fault inputs and independently requires SPI
  counts `[16 24 40 72 136]` and I2C counts `[36 45 63 99 171]`.
- Synthetic I2C stretch `[0 25 100 250] us` changes elapsed time and goodput without changing clocked cells
  or SPI output. It is not an electrical or controller-limit claim.
- A target NACK stops only at target-owned header responses. A register-byte NACK stops after 18 pulses,
  before repeated START/data. A later clean retry exactly recovers the state-free baseline.
- A returned SPI bit flip changes `0x56` to `0x57`; all generic SPI clocks complete with no invented ACK or
  checksum. Device protocols may add detection that this fixture deliberately omits.
- A common deadline accepts an exact elapsed boundary and rejects a meaningfully later result. The fixed
  300 us stretch yields 457.5 us I2C modeled time and misses 250 us while the 100 us SPI fixture passes.
  Rejected results expose no committed payload. This analytical classification is not driver cancellation.
- Calls are state-free; clean inputs are exact rollback, clean re-invocation is retry recovery, and malformed
  calls cannot contaminate later calls.
