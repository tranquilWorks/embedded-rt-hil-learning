## Deterministic graph and failure contract

The baseline fixture is permanent P16 data, not a claim about an external device:

- source values are `0:31` engineering units at timestamps `0:1000:31000 us`;
- the transparent fast output is `[0 0.5 1.5 2.5 ... 30.5]`;
- `D=4`, fast cost `100 us/sample`, slow cost `1200 us/frame`, and timeout `2000 us`;
- eight frame outputs are `[1.125 5 9 13 17 21 25 29]` engineering units;
- release times are `3000:4000:31000 us`, completion times are `4300:4000:32300 us`;
- input/output rates are `1000/250 Hz`, slow utilization is `0.30`, latency is `1300 us`, slack is `700 us`,
  inclusive outstanding depth is one, and no output is late or dropped.

The decimation sweep keeps all costs and policies fixed. Frame counts are `[32 16 8 4]`, output rates are
`[1000 500 250 125] Hz`, utilization is `[1.2 0.6 0.3 0.15]`, timeout counts are `[28 0 0 0]`, maximum latency
is `[7500 1300 1300 1300] us`, and maximum outstanding depth is `[7 1 1 1]` frames.

The cost sweep resets the baseline and keeps `D=4`. Utilization is `[0.05 0.20 0.30 0.45 1.05]`, maximum latency
is `[300 900 1300 1900 5700] us`, minimum slack is `[1700 1100 700 100 -3700] us`, timeout counts are
`[0 0 0 0 8]`, and maximum outstanding depth is `[1 1 1 1 2]` frames.

The deliberately broken consumer-first baseline underflows frame zero and leaves every frame timestamp `1000 us`
stale. Later values are `[4 8 12 16 20 24 28]`, one engineering unit below their correct comparators. With fast
cost `2000 us/sample`, the independent reference instead sees visible counts `[1 3 5 7 9 11 13 15]`, two
underflows, and growing multi-sample timestamp lag. This is a data-version/topological failure, not a deadline
failure.

Independent system-risk review found that the model marked an underflow invalid but still admitted it to the slow
worker. The D=4 broken fixture hid the phantom service because it finished before the next frame. With `D=1`,
consumer-first order, and otherwise baseline costs, the invalid first firing instead delayed the first valid start
from `1000` to `1200 us`, raised late frames from 26 to 27, maximum latency from `7200` to `7400 us`, and maximum
outstanding depth from six to seven. The repair leaves data-ready, slow-start, scheduled-finish, stop, and completion
undefined for the invalid firing, assigns it zero outstanding depth, and excludes it from timeout/cancellation and
worker availability. The first valid frame now starts at `1000 us` and completes at `2200 us`; the independent
behavioral regression and retained MATLAB assertions require 26 late frames, `7200 us` maximum latency, and maximum
outstanding depth six.

At `1900 us` slow cost, scheduled latency equals the `2000 us` timeout and all frames emit. At `1901 us`, strict
lateness cancels every frame under `cancel-frame`. In the overloaded `D=1` case, continue-processing emits all 32
values and marks 28 late with outstanding depth seven; cancellation emits four, cancels 28, and bounds outstanding
depth at two.
Cancellation preserves source, fast, and computed frame values. A clean explicit-input rerun reproduces baseline;
this is computational recovery rather than physical rollback.
