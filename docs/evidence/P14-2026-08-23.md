## Deterministic model and failure contract

P14 reuses 32 P13 codes `mod(37(i-1)+11,4096)`. The producer period is 50 us. A consumer starts at 200 us,
runs every 200 us, and drains up to four records. The counted ring has eight usable slots and declares
consumer-before-producer ordering at equality. The baseline has `rho=1`, occupancy `[1 2 3 4]` repeated,
required and peak capacity four samples, consumed IDs `1:32`, ages `[200 150 100 50]` repeated, zero loss,
zero conservation residual, and final drain at 1600 us.

Capacity `[1 2 3 4 6 8]` yields loss `[24 16 8 0 0 0]`, peak `[1 2 3 4 4 4]`, and consumed counts
`[8 16 24 32 32 32]`. Consumer quota `[1 2 3 4 5 8]` yields load `[400 200 133.333... 100 80 50]%`,
required capacity `[25 18 11 4 4 4]`, loss `[17 10 3 0 0 0]`, and drain
`[3000 2200 2000 1600 1600 1600] us`.

The broken case uses capacity four, quota one, and overwrite-oldest. It accepts all incoming writes and keeps
occupancy bounded, but overwrites 21 unread IDs. Backpressure consumes all IDs only by stalling 27 producer
actions for 4050 us and moving the last decision/drain to 5600/6400 us. A 50-us drain timeout passes by
equality. At 49 us, continuation drains all records; cancellation retains IDs 29:32 as pending with zero loss.

The system-risk review found that observed producer timestamps used repeated addition while the no-loss
capacity reference used the documented `(i-1)T_p` expression. With `T_p=T_c=0.1 us`, consumer start
`0.2 us`, capacity two, quota one, and seven records, binary recurrence drift could move the seventh producer
ahead of an intended consumer tie, fabricate a full encounter/drop, and contradict the reported required
capacity of two. Producer-ready times now use `(i-1)T_p + cumulative backpressure stall`; the independent
behavioral reference and executable MATLAB check retain occupancy `[1 2 2 2 2 2 2]`, consumed IDs `1:7`,
peak/required capacity two, no full encounter, no loss, and completion at `0.8 us`.
